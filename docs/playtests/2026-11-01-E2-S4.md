## Running it

Run `./gradlew runClient`. No extra flag is needed. `build.gradle` adds JEI as `localRuntime` in every
profile (ADR 0001 pin table), so the default `runtime_profile=none` in `gradle.properties` already has it.
There is no JEI profile. `-Pruntime_profile=ultimine` and `-Pruntime_profile=materials` only add those mods,
and neither is used here: step 4 should be vanilla mining with no vein mining.

Run it on a build with the redrawn corner textures. Commit d9413d5 does not have them.

## What it costs

The smallest portal is a 2 by 3 opening with the corners left empty, which takes 10 frames (§3.2; Nb).

- One frame: 8 stone bricks and 1 lapis lazuli, one frame per craft.
- 10 frames: 80 stone bricks and 10 lapis.
- The signet: 1 iron ingot and 1 redstone dust.
- Total: 80 stone bricks, 10 lapis, 1 iron and 1 redstone, before the mod does anything. Redstone needs an iron pickaxe, so a fresh world spends 4 iron, not 1.

The bricks start as 80 stone, which is 800 seconds of smelting in one furnace and less with more. That is the same load the old iron recipe had, so it is not a new brake on getting in.

This is the number to judge. The frame is meant to stay cheap, because portals go up to 21 by 21. If getting in
felt too cheap, that is an answer we act on, by raising the signet's cost.

## Not bugs yet

- Right-clicking a frame with the signet does nothing. Opening a portal arrives with E3.
- The signet's last tooltip line talks about the next tier. Tiers 2 to 4 do not exist yet, so it points at
  nothing for now.
- Breaking a frame with an empty hand drops nothing, and that costs the 8 bricks and the lapis. §3.2 asks for
  it, and vanilla stone bricks do the same. Still say if it surprised you.
- All four sides of a ring show the interlace on their inner and outer faces (§3.2). Say if it looks too busy.

## Steps

1. Run `./gradlew runClient` and wait for the title screen. Check Mods lists Ore Vault.
2. Create a new world: Survival, Allow Commands on. Note the time on a real clock when you land.
3. Open your inventory and search `@orevault` in JEI. Both items show, with their own icons and names, no purple
   cube. Open each recipe: the frame is 8 stone bricks and 1 lapis for one frame, the signet is iron then
   redstone in a row.
4. Play survival, no commands, until you hold 80 stone bricks, 10 lapis, 1 iron ingot and 1 redstone dust. Note
   the time you stop. If you give up first, note what you had and how long it took.
5. Craft one frame the way JEI shows it, then craft one with the lapis in a different slot. It is shapeless, so
   both work. Each craft gives one frame.
6. Craft the other frames until you have 10. You should have the 1 iron and 1 redstone left.
7. Craft the signet: iron on the left, redstone on the right, in a row.
8. Hover the signet. Three lines: "Opens a Vault Frame portal to your team's Vault", then "Right-click a
   finished portal frame to open it", then, fainter, "Craft it into the next tier to add a new ability".
   No raw lang key showing in place of text, nothing cut off.
9. Single block and item in hand: place one frame on its own, clear of any other frame, and walk round it. Every
   face shows the same whole pattern. Hold a frame and look at it in your hand and in the hotbar. It matches the
   lone block. Mine the block back with your pickaxe.
10. No-corner ring: place all 10 frames as a portal ring, a 2 by 3 opening with the corners empty. It should read
    as four separate bars, each with a closed end at its last block, and no seam between the blocks in a bar.
    Look up close and from a few blocks back, then press F2 from the front. Listen for the stone sound.
11. Walk round to the back of the ring and check the same things. Nothing should look mirrored or broken.
12. Right-click a frame with the signet. Nothing should happen (see above).
13. Still in survival, make a wooden pickaxe and break the middle block of an upright bar, then a piece of stone
    nearby. The frame takes about as long as the stone, sounds like stone, and drops one frame. Creative breaks
    instantly and drops nothing, so this has to be survival. The blocks either side of the gap should close
    their ends the moment it goes.
14. With an empty hand, break another frame. It takes several seconds, about as long as stone by hand, and
    drops nothing.
15. Run `/gamemode creative` and find the Ore Vault tab (use the arrows if it is not on the first page). The
    tab icon is the frame, and it holds exactly the two items.
16. Corner ring: mend the ring from the tab and add the four corner blocks, 14 frames in all. It should close
    into one carved border with rounded corners, and the corners should have the same dark bezel as the rest.
    Look from the front and from behind, and press F2 from the front. Then swap one corner for stone: that corner
    goes back to two bar ends, and the stone connects to nothing. Put the frame back.
17. Run seen from behind: build a straight row of 5 frames running east to west, and another running north to
    south, each at eye height and clear of other frames. Look at each from both sides. The pattern should run
    through with no seam, and both ends should close properly from both sides. The top and bottom of a row show
    the interlace too.
18. Thick wall: build a wall 5 wide, 4 tall and 2 thick. The front, back, top and side faces all show the
    interlace, with no seam across the wall. Press F2.
19. Texture check: put a frame, the signet, stone bricks, a lapis block and an iron ingot in your hotbar, and open
    your inventory. Press F2 at GUI Scale 2, 3 and 4 (Options, Video Settings). Then place a 3 by 3 wall of
    frames, one block thick, beside stone bricks and press F2 again.
20. Quit the game. Attach `run/client/logs/latest.log` if anything went wrong, and answer the questions below.
