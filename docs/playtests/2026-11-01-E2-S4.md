## Running it

Run `./gradlew runClient`. No extra flag is needed. `build.gradle` adds JEI as `localRuntime` in every
profile (ADR 0001 pin table), so the default `runtime_profile=none` in `gradle.properties` already has it.
There is no JEI profile. `-Pruntime_profile=ultimine` and `-Pruntime_profile=materials` only add those mods,
and neither is used here: step 4 should be vanilla mining with no vein mining.

## What it costs

The smallest portal is a 2 by 3 opening with the corners left empty, which takes 10 frames (§3.2; Nb).

- One frame: 8 iron ingots and 1 redstone dust, one frame per craft.
- 10 frames: 80 iron and 10 redstone.
- The signet: 1 iron and 1 redstone.
- Total: 81 iron and 11 redstone, before the mod does anything.

This is the number to judge. It is a design choice, not a fixed fact, and a "too much" or "too little" from you
is an answer we act on.

## Not bugs yet

- Right-clicking a frame with the signet does nothing. Opening a portal arrives with E3.
- The signet's last tooltip line talks about the next tier. Tiers 2 to 4 do not exist yet, so it points at
  nothing for now.
- Breaking a frame with an empty hand drops nothing, and that costs the 8 iron. §3.2 asks for it, and vanilla
  iron blocks do the same. Still say if it surprised you.

## Steps

1. Run `./gradlew runClient` and wait for the title screen. Check Mods lists Ore Vault.
2. Create a new world: Survival, Allow Commands on. Note the time on a real clock when you land.
3. Open your inventory and search `vault` in JEI. Both items show, with their own icons and names, no purple
   cube. Open each recipe: the frame is 8 iron and 1 redstone for one frame, the signet is iron then redstone
   in a row.
4. Play survival, no commands, until you hold 81 iron ingots and 11 redstone dust. Note the time you stop. If
   you give up first, note what you had and how long it took.
5. Craft one frame the way JEI shows it, then craft one with the redstone in a different slot. It is
   shapeless, so both work. Each craft gives one frame.
6. Craft the other frames until you have 10. You should have 1 iron and 1 redstone left.
7. Craft the signet: iron on the left, redstone on the right, in a row.
8. Hover the signet. Three lines: "Opens a Vault Frame portal to your team's Vault", then "Right-click a
   finished portal frame to open it", then, fainter, "Craft it into the next tier to add a new ability".
   No raw lang key showing in place of text, nothing cut off.
9. Place all 10 frames as a portal ring: a 2 by 3 opening, corners empty. Look at the block up close and from
   a few blocks back. Listen for the metal sound.
10. Right-click a frame with the signet. Nothing should happen (see above).
11. Still in survival, make a wooden pickaxe and break one frame, then a piece of stone nearby. The frame takes
    about as long as the stone, sounds like metal, and drops one frame. Creative breaks instantly and drops
    nothing, so this has to be survival.
12. With an empty hand, break another frame. It takes several seconds, about as long as stone by hand, and
    drops nothing.
13. Run `/gamemode creative` and find the Ore Vault tab (use the arrows if it is not on the first page). The
    tab icon is the frame, and it holds exactly the two items.
14. Texture check: put a frame, the signet, an iron block, an iron ingot and flint and steel in your hotbar,
    and open your inventory. Press F2 at GUI Scale 2, 3 and 4 (Options, Video Settings). Then place a 3 by 3
    wall of frames beside stone and press F2 again.
15. Quit the game. Attach `run/client/logs/latest.log` if anything went wrong, and answer the questions below.
