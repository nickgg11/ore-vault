## Running it

The whole session runs on the dev dedicated server, so there is one world and one Vault from start to end.
Single player would do for the logout in step 12, but the restart in step 13 needs `runServer`, and building a
second portal there would cost steps. Running on the dedicated server also checks that nothing on the portal
path is client-only.

1. Rename `run/server/world` to `run/server/world-before-E3-S7`, so the Vault is created by this session's
   first portal trip and not restored from an older one. `run/` is untracked.
2. `run/server/eula.txt` needs `eula=true`, and `online-mode=false` is already set in
   `run/server/server.properties`.
3. Start `./gradlew runServer` in one terminal and wait for `Done`. Start `./gradlew runClient` in another and
   join Multiplayer, Direct Connection, `localhost`.

Console input does not reach the server under Gradle's `runServer` (`tools/restart-check/restart-check.ps1`), so
the dev client's player, `Dev` (offline UUID `380df991-f603-344c-a090-369bad2a924a`), is opped in
`run/server/ops.json` before the start, and the server is stopped with `/stop` from chat.

Two logs matter: the server's `run/server/logs/latest.log`, which has the mod's lines, and the client's
`run/client/logs/latest.log`.

The four seconds in step 5 and step 8 are best timed with a phone stopwatch, started the moment you step in.

## Not bugs yet

- There is no ore. The baseline comes at T4, so do not judge the Vault's richness here.
- The door does not swing open as you walk up. That is E3-S13 (#401), after this playtest.
- The wobble overlay is the portal's own texture tinted to its tier, and the signet plays the vanilla trigger
  sound at a quarter volume. Both are placeholders until the mod has its own (#407). Say if they still bother you.
- A wrong frame gives no message, only the failure sound. Like a nether portal, it opens or it does not (§3.2;
  Nick, 2026-10-27, Q6).
- Coming home, you may land a block or two past the far side of the Overworld portal rather than in front of it.
  The return spot is walked out along the way you were walking, past the last portal block, so the portal does
  not fire again (§3.2).
- There is no Tome yet. The T6 check in step 12 is repeated with the Tome in E7-S9.

## Steps

1. Check the server log has `Restored 0 Vaults, skipped 0`. Join, and press F3: you are in survival in the
   Overworld, and `/give` works because `Dev` is opped.
2. Run `/give @s orevault:vault_frame 24`, `/give @s orevault:worn_signet` and `/give @s minecraft:iron_pickaxe`.
   Walk about 30 blocks from spawn to flat ground, so that coming home to the right place means something.
3. Wrong frame: build a ring for a 2 wide by 3 tall opening with the corners empty, but leave out the middle
   block of the top bar (9 frames). No door appears. Right-click a frame with the signet. Pass: the failure sound
   plays and no portal or door appears. Nothing on screen is expected, so check only the sound.
4. Place the missing block. The sealed door fills the opening. Right-click the door with the signet. Pass: the
   door turns into the green portal, the strands in the ring's frames turn green, and the trigger sound is
   quiet.
5. Stand in front of the portal and write down your F3 block position. Step in, and step back out after about
   two seconds: nothing happens and you stay in the Overworld. Then step in and stay. Pass: the green wobble
   lasts about four seconds before the travel sound and the trip.
6. Arrival. Pass: without turning, you face the return portal, green, standing three blocks ahead of you on
   the grass. F3 shows block X 0, Z 0, about Y 251, in `orevault:vault_<id>`. The action bar says "Your team's
   Vault. The portal ahead takes you home." The server log has `Registered Vault ...` and
   `Return portal of ... wrote 6 cells and 14 frames`.
7. Look round in every direction, then dig straight down with the pickaxe through the four blocks of dirt and a
   few blocks into the stone. Pass: everything is in full daylight, the walls at the bottom of the hole are lit
   with no torch, and blocks break at normal speed (a Vault created while the server runs must tick). Set
   Render Distance to 4 in Video Settings and look at the horizon for the fog. Take F2 screenshots of the sky at
   the horizon and of the bottom of the hole. Set Render Distance back.
8. Walk into the return portal. Pass: about four seconds of wobble, then you are back in the Overworld within two
   blocks of the position from step 5, not standing in the portal, and the action bar says "Back from the
   Vault." Stand still for ten seconds: the portal does not fire again.
9. Go back in through the same portal. Pass: you land on the same block, X 0, Z 0, facing the same return
   portal, and there is still only one return portal in sight.
10. Death in the Vault: run `/kill @s`. Pass: the death screen shows the Respawn button within a few seconds.
    Respawn. Pass: you stand at the Overworld spawn, the view is level and the screen normal, and a jump off
    four or more blocks costs you health (you are not invulnerable). Fail: the death screen comes back, the view
    is tilted, or nothing hurts you.
11. Disconnect and join again. Pass: you are where you respawned, not moved home and not in the Vault, and the
    server log has no `logged in ...` line from the mod for this login.
12. T6 check: walk back to your portal and go into the Vault. Walk about ten blocks off the anchor, write down
    your F3 position, then Disconnect and join again. Pass: you are on the same block, inside the Vault, and no
    `logged in ...` line from the mod.
13. Server restart with you inside: still in the Vault, run `/stop`. Your client is
    dropped to the server list. Start `./gradlew runServer` again, wait for `Done`, and check the log has
    `Restored 1 Vaults, skipped 0`. Join again. Pass: you are on the same block inside the Vault, not falling
    through the Overworld sky, the return portal is still there, and a block you mine breaks at normal speed.
14. Walk back to the return portal and go through. Pass: you land within two blocks of the Overworld spot you
    entered from in step 12, so the return position lasted the restart.
15. Run `/stop`. Attach both `latest.log` files if anything went wrong,
    and answer the questions below.
